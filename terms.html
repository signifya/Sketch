1. About this service

Sketch Se Pyaar ("we", "us", "our") creates custom pencil and colour sketch portraits based on a photo you share with us. By placing an order, you agree to the terms on this page.

2. How an order works

  • You select a sketch type (pencil or colour) and number of faces, and complete payment online.
  • After payment, you share your photo and payment screenshot with us on WhatsApp.
  • Your digital sketch is prepared and delivered as a print-ready PDF on WhatsApp, typically within 24 hours of us receiving and confirming your photo.
  • Because every sketch is custom-made from the photo you provide, please share a clear, front-facing photo for the best result. We are not responsible for reduced quality caused by a blurry, dark, or low-resolution photo.

3. Optional physical print & delivery
