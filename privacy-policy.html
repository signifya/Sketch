Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What this policy covers

This page explains what information Sketch Se Pyaar collects when you place an order, how it's used, and how it's protected.

2. What we collect

  • Your photo — shared with us directly over WhatsApp after payment, used solely to create your sketch.
  • Order details — the sketch type, number of faces, and price you select at checkout.
  • Delivery address — only if you choose the optional physical print add-on, used solely to ship your framed print.
  • Contact information — your WhatsApp number, used to communicate about your order.
  • Payment information — payments are handled entirely by our third-party payment partner. We do not receive or store your card, UPI, or full payment details.

Our website itself does not collect or store any photo you may preview using the "upload photo" option on the order page — that preview happens only in your own browser and is never sent to us. Your actual photo is only collected when you personally send it to us on WhatsApp.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and deliver your custom sketch.
  • To ship your physical print, if you've added that option.
  • To communicate with you about your order on WhatsApp.
  • To improve our service and, where you've consented via cookies/pixels, to measure the performance of our ads.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. How long we keep your photo

We retain the photo you send us only for as long as needed to complete your order and provide reasonable after-sales support. You can request that we delete your photo at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.

5. Third-party services we use

  • Payment processing — to securely process your payment.
  • WhatsApp — to collect your photo, share progress updates, and deliver your finished sketch.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel — to understand how our ads are performing. This may collect standard analytics data such as page views and purchase events, in line with Meta's own privacy practices.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp. We'll respond as quickly as we reasonably can.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or WhatsApp is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, message us on WhatsApp at +91 78499 10028.
