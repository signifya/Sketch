Refund Policy

Last updated:

In short

Because every sketch is custom-made specifically from the photo you provide, we do not offer refunds once payment is completed. Please read the details below before ordering.

1. Custom, made-to-order products

Every sketch we create is personalised — drawn specifically from the photo and details you share with us. Unlike ready-made products, it cannot be resold or reused for another customer once created. For this reason, orders are final and non-refundable once payment has been made, regardless of the sketch type or number of faces ordered.

2. Before you order

Please double-check your sketch type (pencil or colour) and number of faces before completing payment, and make sure you're able to share a clear photo afterwards. Once your order is placed, it cannot be cancelled or refunded.

3. The optional framed print add-on

If you choose the physical print & delivery add-on, this is billed as a separate, additional charge on top of your digital sketch. This charge is also non-refundable once paid, as it involves custom printing and framing made specifically for your order.

4. Physical delivery timelines

Physical prints typically take 7 to 10 days to deliver, depending on your location. This is an estimated timeframe, not a guaranteed date — delays from courier partners, weather, or regional logistics are outside our control. A longer-than-expected delivery time on its own is not grounds for a refund, but if your order is significantly delayed, please reach out to us on WhatsApp and we'll help track it down.

5. If something genuinely goes wrong

While we don't offer refunds, if there's a genuine issue on our end — for example, your physical print arrives damaged in transit, or you don't receive your digital sketch at all — please message us on WhatsApp with details and photos where relevant. We'll look into it and do our best to make it right.

6. Questions before you order?

If you're unsure about anything — sizing, delivery to your area, or how the process works — message us on WhatsApp before paying. We're happy to help you decide.

7. Contact us

For any refund or order-related questions, message us on WhatsApp at +91 78499 10028.
